Audio originals are retained under source/. WAV conversion of the compressed
audio uses ffmpeg (mono 44100 Hz signed 16-bit PCM); the original files remain
the preferred source. No upstream game scripts are executed or copied into
the engine. Kick timing/anchors are implemented by Doom64-RTX's render sidecar.

The release's single visuals PK3 includes the converted sounds, kick PNGs,
this source record, the upstream licence and full credits. Gameplay/Audio
settings can disable the upgrades. Missing audio falls back to the ROM synth.
