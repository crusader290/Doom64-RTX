Resource packs bundled with Doom64-RTX (loaded automatically from this folder; turn them
off in Options > Graphics > Texture Packs or with respacks = 0 in doom64rtx.ini).

  doom64rt-materials.pk3     ray tracing materials (normal, roughness/metal, emissive maps,
                             light colours) from Retribution-RT-Materials
  d64r-lostsoul-rt.pk3       lost soul sprites for ray tracing
  d64r-caco-ball-recolor.pk3 cacodemon fireball sprites

Source: doom64-rt by jlrouzies (https://github.com/jlrouzies-fr/doom64-rt, commit
750c1d84f87546de38fe8ad774da59ff6a606ed0), made for Doom 64: Retribution by Nevander.
These files are not covered by Doom64-RTX's GPLv3 licence; the doom64-rt repository ships
no licence file. Included at the Doom64-RTX repository owner's request.
