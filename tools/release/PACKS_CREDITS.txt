Doom64-RTX consolidated visual pack: doom64rtx-visuals.pk3

Contains lost-soul sprites from d64r-lostsoul-rt.pk3, cacodemon fireballs from
 d64r-caco-ball-recolor.pk3, SFLATC and SPACECE ceiling-light textures from
 d64r-bulb-textures.wad, plus normal, roughness/metallic, emissive maps and
 material defaults from Retribution-RT-Materials.

Source: doom64-rt by jlrouzies (https://github.com/jlrouzies-fr/doom64-rt),
commit 750c1d84f87546de38fe8ad774da59ff6a606ed0, for Doom 64: Retribution
by Nevander. These assets are not covered by Doom64-RTX's GPLv3 licence;
the source repository ships no licence file. Included at the repository
owner's request. Per-file origins are recorded in the pack's manifest.json.

Development/quarantine materials, unused height maps, broken texture tests,
GZDoom scripts and replacement maps are omitted from the release pack.
