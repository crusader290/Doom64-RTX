Doom64-RTX @VERSION@, commit @COMMIT@
Requires your own Doom 64 ROM. No ROM is included.

New in 0.5.4:
- Centered main and pause items, original game lettering and animated skull cursor;
  simplified red settings styling. Bottom status bar uses attributed Brutal Doom
  panel/digits with reactive Retribution portraits.
- Unlimited flashlight: battery, recharge, burnout/flicker and flashlight UI removed.
  Hitmarker feedback removed. Ordinary crosshair remains optional.
- Quieter imported effects (0.32 gain), Audio > New Sounds toggle, original sound
  fallback when disabled or missing. Title sting gain 0.5 through Music Volume.
- Native Brutal Doom SMG and assault-rifle variants with original frames/sounds,
  base damage 12/20, faster cadence and ADS spread reduction. Existing pistol and
  chaingun pickup/ammo slots; Gameplay > Imported Weapons restores original guns.
  No magazine/reload scripting, grenade launcher or complete Brutal Doom arsenal.
- Fresh level entry resets pitch/recoil to look straight ahead. Save loading retains
  saved view. Jump grunt plays at takeoff, with silent jump landing; normal fall
  grunts remain. Long jumps off ledges are covered by a native code regression test.
- Seven native sky panoramas selected by native sky type, yaw/pitch and widescreen;
  original sky simulation/RNG preserved. Fire/void/evil skies retain native visuals.
- Native title fade and one-shot imported music sting. Full composite skies, sky
  actors and replacement maps are not interpreted.

Retains crash hardening/logging, heavy bounded gore, key rebinding, aggressive
combat, 120 FPS defaults, native portrait/liquid effects and direct -warp/-skill.
One consolidated PK3 contains compatible art, sounds, descriptors and provenance.

Validation: Linux, Windows cross and native dependency builds; sanitizer suites
for actual weapon/ammo/ADS logic, jump sound timing, unlimited flashlight, PCM,
combat, title/sky and earlier math/gore/bindings/liquid regressions. Real main/pause,
SMG/rifle and Windows hardware RT/audio captures; main-menu Quit exits normally.
Original crash camera unavailable; no exact camera replay claim.

Native adapters, not a GZDoom VM: general ZScript/DECORATE, ACS, UDMF, SBARINFO,
replacement maps and full Brutal Doom gameplay remain unsupported.
See docs/ADDON_COMPATIBILITY.md and bundled asset provenance for exact limits.
