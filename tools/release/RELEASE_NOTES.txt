Doom64-RTX @VERSION@, commit @COMMIT@
Requires your own Doom 64 ROM. No ROM is included.

New in 0.5.2:
- Native ports of persistent blood and monster BloodColor properties. Lifetime
  settings and FIFO limits; permanent stains remain bounded to 128.
- Flashlight battery/HUD and shadowed RT spotlight: F toggles, 30s drain, 15s
  recharge, low-charge flicker, switch/burnout cues. Charge resets on map/load.
- Native Add-ons, Flashlight, Blood Lifetime/Limit settings. Selected upstream
  declarations consolidated into the same single PK3; no extra packs required.
- Key Bindings page: capture 12 movement/combat keys, swap conflicts, cancel,
  restore defaults and save. Core menu/weapon/function shortcuts stay fixed.
- Matching restart/return confirmation menus and accurate contextual prompts.
- Deterministic alphabetical pack discovery and explicit unsupported-descriptor logs.

Retains 0.5.1 boot/recoil animations and four attributed sound imports; 0.5.0 divide
crash fixes, heavy cosmetic gore, aggressive weapons/kick and 120 FPS defaults.

Validation: Linux/Windows builds; sanitizer math, gore, animation, PCM, native
adapter/ABI/battery and binding save/reload tests. GL menu/gameplay smoke; Windows
RT flashlight off/on image comparison and Linux RT smoke. Prior save/load and
weapon crash replays passed. Exact original crash camera was unavailable.

These are native behavior adaptations, not a GZDoom VM. General ZScript/DECORATE
states/ACS/UDMF/SBARINFO and other archived handlers/maps require conversion.
See docs/ADDON_COMPATIBILITY.md for supported behaviors and differences.
Flashlight cone requires RT; modern combat effects are disabled in demos/recording.
