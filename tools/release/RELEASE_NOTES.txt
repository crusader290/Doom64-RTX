Doom64-RTX @VERSION@, commit @COMMIT@
Requires your own Doom 64 ROM. No ROM is included.

New in 0.5.3:
- Native Retribution face HUD: portraits react to damage, death, invulnerability,
  firing and weapon pickups. Clean health/armor/ammo/key and battery layout.
- Native poison bubbles and compatible lava sparks: 48-particle cap, separate
  cosmetic RNG, portal visibility, ceiling/floor collision, growing frames,
  interpolated depth-tested billboards and at most eight small RT lights.
  Native slime floors work; the ROM has no matching HLAVA/D64LAVA lava flats.
- Face HUD and Liquid Effects settings; original art and selected declarations
  consolidated into the same single PK3, with per-file provenance.
- Direct map startup: -warp 1..32 -skill 1..5. Choices are not saved in the ini.

Retains 0.5.2 persistent colored blood, flashlight battery/shadowed RT cone,
key rebinding and confirmation polish; 0.5.1 boot/recoil animations and four
attributed sounds; crash fixes, aggressive combat and 120 FPS defaults.

Validation: seven sanitizer suites, Linux/Windows builds, all 32 native map
startups, MAP01/MAP07 GL and Windows hardware RT on/off captures. 550-frame
environmental on/off gameplay/RNG traces match. Original crash camera unavailable.

Native adaptations, not a GZDoom VM. General ZScript/DECORATE states, ACS,
UDMF/SBARINFO and replacement maps require conversion. Composite sky/title
handlers, original environmental fragment/saturation CVars and diagnostic
add-ons remain unported. See docs/ADDON_COMPATIBILITY.md for exact limits.

F toggles the RT flashlight; V kicks. Settings > Key Bindings changes 12 primary
actions. Arrow/Enter/Esc, weapon number slots and function shortcuts stay fixed.
F5/F9 quicksave/load, F7 debug, F8 RT toggle, F11 fullscreen, F12 capture.
